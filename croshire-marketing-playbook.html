Chapter 1

The Dream Customer Profile

Every marketing dollar must target one specific person. For Croshire Estates Corp, that person is:

  • Real estate investor with 1-10 properties, strong rental income, bank rejected
  • International aviation buyer — UHNW, no US credit, needs asset-based solution
  • Yacht/vessel buyer — Mediterranean, MENA, Caribbean markets
  • Business owner needing $50K-$1.5M, 2+ years in business, bank said no
The dream customer is NOT everyone who needs money. It is the person whose problem we solve best, fastest, and most profitably.
Chapter 2

Lead Magnets — Give Before You Ask

Hormozi's core principle: Give so much value upfront that prospects feel obligated to engage. For Croshire, lead magnets include:

  • Free DSCR loan eligibility check (no credit pull)
  • Aviation finance lender comparison guide
  • Free deal analysis for investment properties
  • Business loan qualification checklist
  • "Bank Said No? Here's Why and What to Do Next" guide
Rule: Every piece of content must be either a Give (educate, help, inspire) or an Ask (apply, call, submit). The ratio must be 4 Gives for every 1 Ask.

Chapter 4

The Grand Slam Offer

Hormozi's Grand Slam Offer combines: Dream Outcome + High Perceived Likelihood + Speed + Ease. For Croshire:

  • Dream Outcome: Funded. Deal closed. Keys in hand.
  • Likelihood: Bank-rejected borrowers welcome. Asset-based. No income verification.
  • Speed: 48-hour decisions (ARF). 7-21 day closings (aviation).
  • Ease: One application. We shop 50+ lenders for you.
"Bank said no? We say yes. One application, 50+ lenders, 48-hour decisions. Asset-based — your income doesn't matter."
