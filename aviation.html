What We Do

Aircraft Consultantage Services

Whether you are selling your aircraft or searching for your next one, we manage the entire transaction on your behalf.

Sellers
Sell Your Aircraft
We list your aircraft across all major aviation marketplaces, handle inquiries, qualify serious buyers, coordinate the pre-purchase inspection, and negotiate on your behalf. You receive offers. We do the work.
Buyers
Find Your Aircraft
Tell us what you need. We search active and off-market listings, review logbooks and maintenance records, arrange inspections, and protect your interests through every stage of the purchase.
Valuation
Know What It's Worth
Using VREF and Aircraft Bluebook alongside live market data, we deliver an accurate valuation before any offer is made. You negotiate with confidence, not guesswork.
Inspection
Pre-Purchase Inspection
We coordinate a licensed A&P mechanic inspection on every aircraft before closing. Every finding is disclosed and negotiated into the final price. No surprises after the deal is done.
Title
FAA Title & Escrow
We run a clean FAA Registry title search, verify no liens exist, and coordinate escrow through trusted aviation title specialists. Every closing is protected and documented.
Fleet
Fleet & International
Acquiring multiple aircraft or sourcing from overseas markets? We manage the full pipeline across North American and European inventory for operators and corporate buyers.

About Us

Built on Relationships. Driven by Results.

Croshire Aviation was founded on one principle -- that buying or selling an aircraft should be simple, transparent, and stress-free.

We are a commission-only consultantage. That means we have no upfront fees, no listing charges, and no retainers. Our success is tied directly to yours. We earn only when your deal closes.

From single engine pistons to corporate jets, we manage every step of the transaction -- valuation, marketing, buyer qualification, pre-purchase inspection, title search, and escrow coordination. You focus on flying. We handle everything else.

"We don't just connect buyers and sellers. We protect both sides of the deal from first contact to final closing."

— Croshire Aviation
